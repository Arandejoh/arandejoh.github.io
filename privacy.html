BLACK — privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Who uses it

BLACK is a private assistant used only by its owner. It has no other users and does not accept sign-ups.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, BLACK reads and updates the owner's own Google Calendar, so it can say what is planned and add or remove events when the owner asks.

Where the data goes

Calendar information is used only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models. BLACK's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove BLACK's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
